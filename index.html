About Praxis

Praxis is the Greek word for practice: knowledge turned into action. It’s a homework planner built around your real schedule, so deadlines line up with the classes that set them.

Clio, the muse of history, is your study guide. She reads your schedule, knows what’s due, and gives you a plan for tonight.
